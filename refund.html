Refund Policy

This page explains how customers can request help with a WORLD TV purchase. Eligibility may depend on the product or service purchased, its status, and applicable consumer law.

Requesting a refund

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, received the wrong product, or experienced a purchase-related problem, contact WORLD TV through the official support details shown on the website and include your order or payment reference.

Digital subscriptions and activation codes

Requests involving digital subscriptions or activation codes are reviewed according to whether the subscription or code has already been activated, delivered, redeemed, or used. We may ask for information needed to verify the transaction and investigate the issue.

Physical products

For physical products, contact support promptly if an item arrives damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered. Keep the product, packaging, and proof of purchase while the request is reviewed.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned through an available supported method. Processing time can vary depending on the original payment provider or financial institution.

Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that customers may have under applicable consumer-protection law.

Questions

For a refund or purchase-support request, use the official WORLD TV contact information published on this website.
