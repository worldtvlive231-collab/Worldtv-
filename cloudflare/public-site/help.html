Frequently asked questions

Can I download the Android app now?

The new official APK download is being checked for integrity and device compatibility. Please visit Android download information. Do not install unverified APK files.

Which devices can I use?

WORLD TV is intended for compatible Android TVs, Google TVs, Android TV boxes, Android phones and tablets. iPhones and iPads are not currently supported.

What happened to the login and registration pages?

They are temporarily unavailable while authentication, email verification, security protections and existing-customer continuity are tested. Keep your existing account details and contact support rather than creating a replacement account.

Where can I pay for a subscription?

New online payment functions are not enabled on this temporary public website. We are completing the Ghana Pocketi and worldwide Paystack verification systems before publishing payment pages. Do not pay unknown intermediaries claiming to activate accounts on behalf of WORLD TV.

What about a payment I already made?

Contact support from your registered WORLD TV email. For a Pocketi payment, include your transaction reference, amount paid and payment date. We will verify the transaction in merchant records before any activation. Never send a payment PIN, full card number or password.

What is the status of admin and reseller access?

The protected administrator and reseller systems are being tested separately. Admin credentials and subscription codes must never be shared publicly. Contact support for existing reseller inquiries.

System restoration status

The public WORLD TV information website is active. The customer portal, manual payment confirmation workflow, verified Android downloads, admin dashboard, reseller panel and customer support chat are being restored in stages.
