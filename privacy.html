World TV Privacy Notice

This is a starter privacy notice and should be reviewed before public launch.

Information We Collect

The website may collect account details, contact information, subscription and order records, support messages and information needed to provide requested services.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to manage accounts, subscriptions, orders, customer support, security, service communications and business records.

Payments

Online payment processing is not currently enabled. When a payment provider is connected, payment-card information should be handled by that provider rather than stored directly by World TV.

Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures should be used to protect customer information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the World TV Contact page.
